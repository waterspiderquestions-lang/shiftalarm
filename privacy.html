Privacy Policy · ShiftAlarm

Effective date: July 27, 2026

The short version

ShiftAlarm collects no personal data. Everything you enter — your schedule, alarms, pay settings — stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

Your shifts, alarm settings, pay settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. You can also export or delete it yourself anytime from the app's backup screen.

What leaves your device

Exactly one thing, and only if you turn on the optional snow early-wake feature: your approximate location (either the phone's coarse location or a location you type in) is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a weather forecast service, to fetch the snowfall forecast. This request contains no name, account, or identifier — just coordinates. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that lookup.

If you never enable snow early-wake, the app makes no network requests with any of your information.

Permissions and why the app asks

  • Alarms & reminders — to ring your alarms at the exact time.
  • Notifications — to show bedtime reminders and alarm alerts.
  • Location (coarse, optional) — only for the snow forecast feature above.
  • Bluetooth (optional) — only if you enable the feature that cancels backup alarms when your phone connects to your car.
  • Calendar (optional) — only if you enable calendar sync.

Children

The app is a utility with no account or content features; it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
